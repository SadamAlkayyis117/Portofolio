ABOUT ME

Sadam Alkayyis

Creative Developer × UI/UX Designer

Hello, I'm Sadam.

I am an Informatics graduate with a strong interest in game development, UI/UX design, and creative digital projects. I enjoy combining programming and visual design to create interactive experiences that are functional, engaging, and visually appealing.

My experience includes developing interactive applications and games, designing user interfaces and prototypes, as well as creating various digital and graphic design projects. I am particularly interested in projects where technology and creativity can work together.

What I Do

Game Development UI/UX Design Prototyping Creative Development Graphic Design

My Approach

I like learning by building things. Whether it is a game, interface, prototype, or visual project, I focus on understanding the problem first and then turning the idea into something that people can actually interact with.

This portfolio is designed in the same spirit. Instead of simply presenting a collection of projects, I wanted to create an interactive environment where you can explore my work like a small game world.
